## Route Overview

This section covers 535km of coastal Pacific riding through three countries: El Salvador, Honduras, and Nicaragua. The route traces the Gulf of Fonseca tri-national waters, includes a unique island ferry crossing to Honduras, and concludes in Granada, Nicaragua's colonial jewel on Lake Nicaragua.

| 113 | Wed Dec 3 | Usulutan → La Union | 90 km | 1,047 m | Paved |

## Stage 112: El Tunco to Usulutan
**Tuesday, December 2, 2025 | 119 km | 784m elevation gain**

### Departing El Tunco

El Tunco (13.494, -89.384) is El Salvador's premier surf town, part of the "Surf City" coastal designation. The stage departs from this black-sand beach community that hosts world-class breaks.

**Morning Pre-Ride**
- Early start recommended before heat intensifies
- Last chance for premium pupusas at local stands
- Famous surf breaks: La Bocana (left break), El Sunzal (right point break)
- Punta Roca (10km down coast) - considered El Salvador's best wave

**Nearby Attractions (if time allows)**
- Cascadas de Tamanique Waterfall (day trip option)
- El Zonte beach town (15 min west) - mellower surf scene
- Mizata beaches (45 min west) - uncrowded surf

### Route Through La Libertad Department

The coastal highway passes through El Salvador's primary Pacific corridor. Expect hot, humid conditions typical of coastal lowlands.

### Arriving Usulutan

Usulutan (13.341, -88.418) is El Salvador's fifth-largest city, capital of Usulutan Department in the agricultural southeast. The city serves as a commercial hub for the surrounding fertile valley.

**City Context**
- Population: ~70,000
- Known as a traditional commercial center
- Damaged by 2001 earthquakes and Hurricane Mitch (1998)
- Main Catholic Church located in central park area
- Recently modernized with national chain restaurants

**Nearby Natural Attractions**
- Laguna de Alegria (volcanic crater lake)
- Bahia de Jiquilisco (UNESCO Biosphere Reserve) - mangroves and wildlife
- Eastern Pacific coastal beaches
- Rich agricultural valley setting

## Stage 113: Usulutan to La Union
**Wednesday, December 3, 2025 | 90 km | 1,047m elevation gain**

This stage traverses eastern El Salvador toward the Gulf of Fonseca, finishing at the tri-national maritime gateway.

### La Union & Gulf of Fonseca

La Union (13.326, -87.825) is El Salvador's principal Pacific port city, positioned on the Gulf of Fonseca where three nations meet the Pacific.

**Geographic Significance**
- Gulf of Fonseca: 1,800 km² tri-national body of water
- Shared by El Salvador, Honduras, and Nicaragua
- 65 km inland reach, 32 km entrance width
- Named after Archbishop Juan Fonseca (discovered 1522)
- El Salvador has 29 km of the 261 km total coastline

**Gulf Islands (Salvadoran territory)**
- Isla Meanguera and Meanguerita (awarded to El Salvador, 1992 ICJ ruling)
- Isla Conchaguita
- Isla Zacatillo
- Isla Martin Perez
- Isla Conchagua (mainland connection)

**Activities & Viewpoints**
- Mirador Espiritu de la Montaña (Conchagua Volcano) - spectacular gulf views
- Boat tours through island archipelago (1-4 hours available)
- Thermal waters in gulf (natural phenomenon)
- Isla Zacatillo: El Carey Beach, birding at Isla de los Pájaros
- Punta Jaguey restaurant - views of all three countries

**Cultural Sites**
- Familia Park (marina area) - boat operators gather here
- Iglesia de Nuestra Señora de las Mercedes (historic church)
- Las Pitas thermal baths
- Central market

**Volcanic Backdrop**
- Conchagua Volcano rises from shore (dominant feature)
- Viewpoints accessible by vehicle/motorbike
- Sunset viewing highly recommended

## Stage 114: La Union to Amapala (Isla del Tigre)
**Thursday, December 4, 2025 | 18 km cycling + water taxi crossing**

### Border Crossing: El Salvador to Honduras

This unique stage involves a short ride to the water taxi terminal, followed by a boat crossing of the Gulf of Fonseca to Honduras' Isla del Tigre.

**Logistics**
- Water taxi from La Union to Coyolito/Amapala
- Crossing time: ~2 hours through tri-national waters
- Immigration at La Union before departure (El Salvador exit)
- Immigration upon arrival in Amapala (Honduras entry)
- Bikes transported on boat
- Views of three countries during crossing

**During Crossing**
- Pass near Isla Conejo (disputed territory, now Salvadoran)
- Views of volcanic archipelago
- Pelicans, osprey, and marine birds common
- Warm Pacific waters

### Isla del Tigre (Tiger Island)

Amapala (13.296, -87.653) is the main town on Isla del Tigre, a 783m conical volcanic island in the Gulf of Fonseca. Population ~2,500.

**Historical Significance**
- Founded by Honduras in 1833
- Only official Pacific port during Republican era (until port moved to San Lorenzo)
- Used as pirate base by Sir Francis Drake and others (raiding Spanish gold ships)
- US radar/radio base during Contra war (1980s, now abandoned)
- Honduras President Marco Aurelio Soto took oath here (1876)
- Awarded to Honduras by ICJ ruling (1992)

**Island Features**
- Cerro El Tigre: 783m dormant volcano at island center
- Black sand volcanic beaches (approximately 20 beaches)
- One 17km coastal road circling island
- Small fishing communities around perimeter
- Traditional red, yellow, blue, and turquoise wooden houses

**Beaches**
- Playa El Burro (east of town) - calmest for swimming, faces mainland
- Playa Grande (nine o'clock position) - most popular, faces El Salvador
- Playa Negra (southwest corner) - prettiest black sand beach
- Playa Caracol, Islita, Gualora, Punta Honda (around island circuit)

**Transportation on Island**
- Moto-taxis available
- Minibuses circle island route
- Walking/cycling the 17km loop possible
- Rates slightly higher than mainland

**Activities**
- Viewpoints (miradores) with views of three countries
- Boat tours to neighboring islands
- Visit Meanguera Island (Salvadoran, lunch stops)
- Isla Meanguerita or Isla de los Pájaros (Bird Island) for birding
- Swimming, fishing from beaches
- Exploring small communities

**Historical Sites**
- Colonial architecture in Amapala
- Old dock area (recently renovated)
- Small tourism office at main dock
- Traditional market and church at town square
- Hondutel call center

**Note**: This is a quiet, slow-paced island. Much of infrastructure dating to its port heyday. Government has plans for future deep-water port development.

## Stage 115: Puerto Potosi, Honduras to Leon, Nicaragua
**Friday, December 5, 2025 | 118 km | 802m elevation gain | 10-20% unpaved**

### Border Crossing: Honduras to Nicaragua

This stage begins with a boat crossing from Isla del Tigre to mainland Honduras at Puerto Potosi (13.009, -87.506), followed by a land border crossing into Nicaragua.

**Route Profile**
- Ferry from Amapala/Coyolito to Puerto Potosi
- Ride through southern Honduras
- Land border crossing into Nicaragua
- Unpaved sections: 10-20% of route
- Finish in Leon, Nicaragua's intellectual capital

### Leon, Nicaragua

Leon (12.436, -86.882) is Nicaragua's second-largest city and the heart of liberal Nicaragua. Founded 1524, the city is a UNESCO World Heritage treasure with profound cultural significance.

**Historical Context**
- Founded by Francisco Hernández de Córdoba (1524)
- Historic rival of Granada for power (centuries of conflict)
- Burned by William Walker (1856) during civil war
- Rebuilt as textbook Spanish colonial city
- Center of religious institutions and education
- Revolutionary history (anti-Somoza resistance)

**UNESCO World Heritage Site**
- Cathedral-Basilica of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary
- Largest cathedral in Central America
- Built 1747-1814 (over 100 years construction)
- Designed by Guatemalan architect Diego José de Porres Esquivel
- Baroque and Neoclassical blend (eclectic style)
- Famous white facade contrasts with Granada's colorful buildings

**Cathedral Highlights (12.435, -86.878)**
- Rooftop access for panoramic city views ($3 USD / 90 Cordobas)
- See Mombacho Volcano, smoking volcanoes, Lake Managua
- Tomb of Rubén Darío (Nicaragua's greatest poet, 1867-1916)
- Guarded by sorrowful lion, "Nicaragua is made for freedom" inscription
- Tombs of poets Alfonso Cortés, Salomón de la Selva
- El Cristo Negro de Pedrarias (possibly oldest Catholic image in Americas, 1528)
- Stations of the Cross by Antonio Sarria (masterpieces)
- 27 crypts beneath designed to survive earthquakes
- La Libertad bell (announced Central America's independence, 1821)
- Historic cannons from 1824 siege
- Tunnel system connects to other churches (anti-pirate construction)

**Ticket Purchase**: Back side of cathedral before 5pm, entrance on left side

**Other Colonial Churches**
- Iglesia de la Recolección (1786) - considered city's most beautiful, Mexican baroque
- Iglesia San Juan Bautista de Subtiaba
- Iglesia La Merced
- Iglesia Guadalupe
- Walk Calle La Libertad for church-to-church exploration

**Museums & Culture**
- Museo de Arte Fundación Ortiz-Gurdián - finest contemporary art in Central America
- Museo Enrique B. Mántica - archaeology museum
- Galería de Héroes y Mártires - FSLN veterans museum with murals
- Museo de la Revolución - rooftop views of cathedral
- Museo Rubén Darío - poet's birthplace
- University of León - architecture and tourism degrees

**Parks & Public Spaces**
- Parque Rubén Darío - statue of poet, busts of Leonese poets
- Various plazas throughout historic center

**Nearby Attractions**
- Flor de Caña rum distillery in Chichigalpa (est. 1890) - world-class rum
- Ruins of León Viejo (45 min drive) - original colonial settlement
- Juan Venado Island Reserve (20km barrier island) - 300m wide, wildlife, birding
- Playa Las Penitas beach

**Active Volcanoes**
- Cerro Negro Volcano - famous for volcano boarding
- Telica Volcano - accessible for viewing
- Momotombo Volcano - visible from city

**Shopping & Markets**
- Mercado Central - local market experience

**Practical Notes**
- Colonial architecture, whitewashed buildings
- Hot climate, early starts recommended
- Very walkable historic center
- Rich revolutionary history (Sandinista stronghold)
- Intellectual and cultural capital of Nicaragua

## Stage 117: Pochomil to Granada + Rest Day
**Sunday, December 7, 2025 | 95 km | 1,266m elevation gain | 1-5% unpaved**
**Monday, December 8, 2025 | Rest Day in Granada**

### Arriving Granada

Granada (11.935, -85.955) is Nicaragua's most beautiful colonial city and the oldest city in mainland Americas (founded 1524). This stage finishes at one of Central America's most charming destinations.

**Historical Significance**
- Founded 1524 by Francisco Hernández de Córdoba
- One of oldest cities in the New World
- Historic rival of Leon for centuries
- Direct water route to Caribbean via Lake Nicaragua and Rio San Juan
- Wealthy trading city despite inland location
- Ransacked multiple times by pirates (including Henry Morgan)
- Burned to ground by William Walker (1856) during civil war
- Rebuilt as textbook Spanish colonial city
- Granada nickname: "La Gran Sultana"

**Colonial Architecture**
- Rainbow of colored buildings (pink, blue, green, yellow, orange)
- Cobblestone streets throughout historic center
- Red tile roofs on Spanish colonial buildings
- Contrast with Leon's whitewashed aesthetic
- Two-story buildings with balconies around plazas

**Catedral de Granada (Catedral Inmaculada Concepción de María)**
- Iconic bright yellow/marigold cathedral (11.934, -85.956)
- Built 1583, rebuilt several times
- Terracotta-colored domes
- Faces Parque Central
- Visible from throughout city
- Symbol of Granada

**Churches & Religious Sites**
- Iglesia de la Merced (Calle Real Xalteva) - oldest religious church in Central America
  - Bell tower climb ($1 USD / 35 Cordobas)
  - Spiral staircase to 360-degree views
  - Views of cathedral, Lake Nicaragua, Volcan Mombacho
  - Best at sunset (last entry 5:30pm, closed by 6pm)
- Iglesia Xalteva
- Iglesia Guadalupe
- Chapel Maria Auxiladora
- Two-kilometer church walk: Maria Auxiladora to Guadalupe via multiple churches

**Parque Central**
- Heart of the city, lively gathering spot
- Food stands selling Nicaraguan delicacies
- Horse-drawn carriages line park
- Perfect for people watching
- Obelisk honoring 1821 independence from Spain
- Surrounded by pastel colonial buildings

**Calle La Calzada**
- Main pedestrian street running from Parque Central to lakeshore
- Restaurant, bar, and nightlife hub
- Lined with cafes, shops, tour operators, artisan bakeries
- Evening atmosphere
- 1.5 km to waterfront

**Lake Nicaragua Waterfront**
- Malecon de Granada - small promenade with park
- City pier for boat tours
- "Granada" letters for photos
- Raspado (shaved ice) carts
- Bicycle rentals available
- Centro Turistico beach area (Santa Cruz)

**Las Isletas de Granada (11.929, -85.890)**
- 365 volcanic islets created by Mombacho eruption 20,000 years ago
- Kayaking tours through jungle-clad waterways
- Boat tours available from marina
- Monkeys visible in trees
- Rich birdlife
- Some islands inhabited (luxury private residences)
- Some islands have eco-lodges
- Fort ruins on some islands

**Markets & Shopping**
- Mercado Municipal - authentic local market (south of town)
  - Chaotic, colorful, bustling
  - Fresh produce, cheese, meats
  - Local food culture
- Masaya Artisan Market (day trip) - handicrafts, hammocks, blankets
  - Note: tourist-oriented pricing

**Museums & Culture**
- Casa de los Tres Mundos (Casa de los Leones) - cultural arts center
  - Beautiful building and gardens
  - Artwork displays
  - 20 Cordobas entry
  - Monday-Saturday 8am-6pm
- ChocoMuseo - cacao and chocolate museum
  - History of chocolate in Nicaragua
  - Manufacturing processes

**Plazas & Public Spaces**
- Parque Central - main square
- Plaza Xalteva (west side) - starting point for walking tours

**Traditional Nicaraguan Food**
- Vigorón - Granada's famous dish
  - Fried pork skins (chicharrones)
  - Cabbage salad (curtido)
  - Boiled yucca
  - Wrapped in banana leaf
  - Available at food stands in Parque Central
- Gallo pinto - rice and beans (breakfast staple)
- Pupusas
- Fresh lake/ocean seafood

**Nearby Day Trip Attractions**

**Laguna de Apoyo (11.925, -85.987)**
- Volcanic crater lake (20 min from Granada)
- Perfect for swimming, kayaking, paddle boarding
- Crystal clear waters
- Hostels and hotels on lakeshore (day passes available)
- Paradiso Hostel - party atmosphere
- Casa Marimba - tranquil, excellent restaurant
- Meditation and wellness activities
- Inner tubes, floating docks

**Volcan Masaya (11.984, -86.168)**
- Active volcano with visible lava
- Evening tours to see glowing red lava in crater
- Santiago crater (reopened after 2024 volcanic activity closure)
- One of most accessible active volcanoes in world
- Smoking crater experience
- Most sought-after experience in Nicaragua

**Volcan Mombacho (11.826, -85.982)**
- Forest-covered dormant volcano looming over Granada
- Hiking trails through cloud forest
- Zipline tours available
- Coffee plantations on slopes
- Views of Granada, Lake Nicaragua, and islets

**La Polvora Fortress**
- Built 1740s to protect city from pirates
- Historical military architecture
- City views

**Entertainment**
- Horse-drawn carriage tours (~$10/hour for 4 people)
  - Romantic way to explore city
  - Tours depart from Parque Central
- Live music venues
- Jesper Lassen - The Danish Mentalist shows (6pm & 9pm)

**Practical Information**
- Very hot and humid (year-round warm weather)
- Walkable historic center
- December is dry season (ideal weather)
- Bicycle rentals widely available (~$3/day)
- Shared shuttles to other Nicaragua destinations
- Buses to Masaya, Managua, Rivas (frequent service)
- Currently much less gentrified post-2018 protests and pandemic
- More authentic, local atmosphere
- Tourism dollars highly valued

**Cultural Notes**
- Bastion of conservatism (less Sandinista graffiti than Leon)
- Elegant, refined colonial charm
- Warm, friendly population
- Laid-back atmosphere
- Colonial streets invite wandering and getting lost

## Regional Context: Pacific Central America

### Border Crossings & Logistics

This tour section involves two significant border crossings:

**El Salvador → Honduras (Water)**
- Unique water taxi crossing of Gulf of Fonseca
- Immigration in La Union (exit El Salvador)
- Immigration in Amapala (enter Honduras)
- Approximately 2-hour boat ride
- Bikes transported on vessel
- Weather-dependent crossing

**Honduras → Nicaragua (Land)**
- Traditional land border crossing
- Immigration procedures at border station
- Currency change: Honduras Lempira → Nicaragua Cordoba
- Note: US Dollar widely accepted in all three countries

### Climate Considerations

**December Weather**
- Dry season throughout region
- Hot, humid coastal conditions
- Cooler in Leon and Granada (inland, higher elevation)
- Strong sun exposure on coastal stages
- Early morning starts highly recommended
- Afternoon heat can be intense
- Lower humidity than rainy season

### Cultural Context

**Language**
- Spanish throughout all three countries
- Regional accents and vocabulary vary
- Indigenous influences in place names

**Food**
- Pupusas dominant in El Salvador
- Seafood emphasis in coastal areas
- Vigorón unique to Granada
- Gallo pinto (rice and beans) ubiquitous in Nicaragua
- Fresh tropical fruits abundant
- Corn-based tortillas and dishes

**Religious Heritage**
- Strong Catholic presence
- Colonial churches in every town
- Religious festivals and traditions
- Indigenous syncretism in some practices

**Political History**
- Civil wars and revolutions (1980s)
- Current political situations vary by country
- Tourism infrastructure rebuilt post-conflict
- Leon and Granada: historic political rivals
- Sandinista vs. Conservative divide in Nicaragua

### Cycling Considerations

**Road Conditions**
- Mostly paved coastal highway
- Stage 115: 10-20% unpaved sections
- Stage 117: 1-5% unpaved
- Heat management critical
- Hydration essential
- Early starts beat afternoon heat
- Gulf coast: flat to rolling
- Inland: more climbing

**Logistics**
- Three currencies: US Dollar, Honduras Lempira, Nicaragua Cordoba
- ATMs in major cities
- Credit cards accepted in tourist areas
- Small bills essential for street vendors
- Water readily available
- Bike shops in larger cities
- Tour support essential for border crossings

## Safety & Health Notes

**General Safety**
- El Salvador: Greatly improved safety since 2019 gang crackdown
- El Tunco: Safe tourist destination
- La Union: Exercise standard precautions
- Leon: Safe, walkable city
- Granada: Safe in tourist areas, less crowded than pre-2018

**Health Considerations**
- Mosquito-borne illnesses possible (dengue, Zika, chikungunya)
- Insect repellent recommended
- Sunscreen essential
- Hydration critical in heat
- Food safety: stick to busy establishments
- Water: bottled or purified recommended
- Vaccinations: consult travel clinic

**Water Safety**
- Swimming at beaches: rip currents possible
- Gulf of Fonseca: warm, calm waters
- Lake Nicaragua: bull sharks present (rare attacks)
- Laguna de Apoyo: very safe for swimming

## Historical Timeline: Granada vs. Leon Rivalry

**1524**: Both cities founded by Francisco Hernández de Córdoba
**1600s-1800s**: Centuries of rivalry for political power
- Leon: Liberal, intellectual, revolutionary
- Granada: Conservative, wealthy, traditional
**1850s**: Granada makes play for leadership
- Leon recruits American mercenary William Walker
- Walker defeats Granada, declares himself president of Nicaragua
- Walker later overthrown
**1856**: Walker burns Granada to ground on retreat
- City left with sign: "Here was Granada"
**1800s-present**: Cities rebuild and develop distinct identities
- Leon: University town, revolutionary spirit, Sandinista stronghold
- Granada: Tourist destination, colonial beauty, conservative bastion
**Today**: Both UNESCO World Heritage Sites, peaceful coexistence

## Weather Forecast: Early December

**Expected Conditions**
- Dry season (minimal rain)
- Daytime highs: 32-35°C (90-95°F) coastal
- Daytime highs: 28-32°C (82-90°F) inland cities
- Evening lows: 22-25°C (72-77°F)
- Humidity moderate (lower than rainy season)
- Prevailing offshore morning winds (coastal)
- Clear skies typical

**Cycling Strategy**
- Start rides at dawn (cooler, less traffic)
- Seek shade during peak heat (11am-3pm)
- Hydration every 15-20 minutes
- Electrolyte replacement important
- Sun protection essential (sunscreen, arm sleeves, helmet vents)
- Rest day in Granada timed well for recovery
